## MY TASTE PROFILE
I rate highly: tense social dramas, offbeat/anxious romance, smart low-budget sci-fi, stylish crime thrillers, and a few comfort comedies with real craft.
I rate medium: glossy studio thrillers and franchise entries that are fun but hollow.
I rate low: broad underwritten comedies, empty holiday formula, prestige films that feel like homework.
I rewatch: Shiva Baby, Coherence, Drive, Palm Springs, My Cousin Vinny, The Others.
Best nights alone: Moon, Nightcrawler, The Lighthouse.
Best with friends: Game Night, Ready or Not, Wild Tales.
When tired: avoid Brutalist-length prestige; pick Palm Springs or Last Holiday instead.
Turkish / local notes I care about: LCV felt closer than festival-flex cinema; some local comedies bounced hard.

## DIRECTOR CLUSTER: THOMAS VINTERBERG
Films in my list: The Hunt, The Celebration, Submarino.
Across them I notice: social systems crushing individuals, ugly honesty, handheld moral pressure.
If I ask for "more Vinterberg energy," start with The Hunt then The Celebration. Watchlist extension: The Commune.

## DIRECTOR CLUSTER: SMART SCI-FI / REALITY GLITCH
Films: Moon, Coherence, Looper, Palm Springs, Upgrade.
Shared taste signal: idea-driven, character-first, not just CGI flex.
If recommending from my list for "mind-bend but emotional," these are the pool.
